## About Me
Hello! I'm **Myint Myat Pyae Sone**, a passionate software engineering student and tech enthusiast from Myanmar.  
I’m currently diving into **DevOps** with a focus on **Linux** through the 'Introduction to Linux' course by the Linux Foundation.  
I also have a strong foundation in **Java** and I'm expanding my skills with **Spring Boot** and improving my code quality with **SOLID** principle and **Design Patterns** to teach myself how to write concise and clean code.

I’ve worked on projects like a **Java EE banking system** and am exploring **FullStack development** using the **MERN stack**.  
I’m passionate about contributing to **open-source projects** and continuously learning. Improving my **English** is also important to me for global collaboration.

Feel free to check out my repositories and connect!
